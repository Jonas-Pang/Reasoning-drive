You get two images of the same moment. Photo 1 is the whole dash-camera photo. Photo 2 is a close-up of the road just ahead of my car, cut from Photo 1 and shown larger; it shows the same paint, only bigger. Look at Photo 1 first, then at Photo 2, then compare them.
Work in seven steps and write each step in its answer fields before the next one. Observation comes before interpretation: never report a feature you cannot see. When a feature is hidden, worn, too dark or too far away, say so and lower the evidence level instead of guessing. The final actions come last and must follow from what you wrote before them.
1. Scene (fields "scene", "lane_count", "ego_lane_index", "scene_evidence"), from Photo 1: say what kind of road this is and what is ahead (an intersection, a ramp, a lane that ends), count the lanes of my direction near the bottom of the photo, and give the position of my lane counted from the left (1 = leftmost lane of my direction).
2. Boundaries (fields "left_boundary", "right_boundary"): for each side, write in "observation" first what Photo 1 shows and then what Photo 2 shows, then fill in the attributes from those observations only. In each photo describe the line next to my car and the line further ahead separately. Count the lines on the side: one line or two side by side. For a broken line, say how many dashes and gaps you can see and how long the gaps are. Say the colour of the paint and whether there is a raised curb or barrier. Compare the left and the right side, so that a line of a neighbouring lane is not taken for a boundary of my lane. Judge the colour from the paint, not from the position: a line on the left is not yellow because it is on the left. Judge the pattern from a stretch long enough to show at least one dash and one gap. Next to my car, Photo 2 decides; a change further ahead (a broken line that becomes solid, a line that ends or begins) is read from Photo 1.
3. Markings (field "lane_markings"): list every arrow, word, symbol, area and line across my lane, from the bottom of the photo to the end of my lane (its stop line, or where it ends), plus a waiting area ahead of my lane and a two-way left-turn lane directly to its left. Look in Photo 2 for words painted in my lane, left-turn and right-turn arrows, and the markings of a waiting area; read arrows and words further ahead from Photo 1. In each "observation" say in which photo you saw the marking. Read an arrow from its head or heads, not from the lane it is in. Write an empty list if nothing is painted in my lane.
4. Cross-check (field "cross_check"): answer each question from your own observations, and correct steps 1 to 3 if an answer shows a contradiction.
   close_up: where Photo 1 and Photo 2 show something different (the colour, the pattern or the number of lines of a boundary, a curb, a word, an arrow or a waiting area), which photo decides (next to my car: Photo 2; changes further ahead and markings far ahead: Photo 1) and what you corrected; or "the same in both photos".
