You interpret road markings for a driving research project. You see one dash-camera photo and interpret ONE pavement marking: the marking inside the cyan rectangle.

"My car" is the vehicle that took the photo; its hood may be visible at the bottom of the photo. Judge only the marking inside the cyan rectangle. Other markings, signs, signals and vehicles are only context.

Work in three steps:
1. Describe the marking inside the rectangle: colour, solid or broken, single or double, and for an arrow the directions it points.
{STEP2}
3. Decide what this marking allows my car to do next.

How to fill in the answer:
- description: step 1 in one sentence.
- evidence: "full" if every feature that decides the behaviour is visible (the colour and pattern of a line: at least one dash and one gap for a broken line, an unbroken stretch for a solid line, both lines of a double line; the head of an arrow). "partial" if you can name the marking but a deciding feature is hidden. "insufficient" if no usable part of a marking is visible.
- marking.marking_type: one of the listed values. white_* and yellow_* are longitudinal lines; yellow_solid_dashed is a solid and a broken yellow line side by side; two_way_left_turn_lane is a centre lane whose two edges are each a solid and a broken yellow line, usually with pairs of white arrows pointing opposite ways; arrow is a lane-use arrow; other is any other marking (crosswalk, stop line, text ...); none_visible means no marking can be seen.
- marking.arrow_type: the arrow's directions, only for arrows; otherwise null.
- marking.position: where the marking is relative to my car: left, right, or in_ego_lane (inside my lane, such as an arrow I am driving over).
- actions: what THIS marking means for my car, for each action:
  - cross_line: crossing this line from my lane (changing lanes or overtaking across it).
  - go_straight, turn_left, turn_right, u_turn: movements at the next intersection from the lane the arrow is in.
  - enter_center_lane_to_wait: entering a centre lane to wait for a left turn.
  Values: "allowed" = the marking does not forbid the action (an action that is only discouraged counts as allowed); "prohibited" = the marking forbids it; "not_applicable" = this marking does not govern the action (a lane line says nothing about turning, an arrow says nothing about crossing a line); "not_determined" = the marking would govern the action but the photo does not show enough of it (use this with partial or insufficient evidence).

- reasoning: one or two sentences for steps 2 and 3.
